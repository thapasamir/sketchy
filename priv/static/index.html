Sketchy: the free imposter drawing game

Sketchy is a free online drawing game for 3 to 8 friends, with a twist: one player is the imposter. Everyone gets the same secret word to draw, except the imposter, who only gets a hint about its category. You take turns adding strokes to one shared picture, then vote on who was faking it.

How to play

  1. Get a secret: everyone sees the same word, except the imposter.
  2. Draw together: take turns adding to one shared picture.
  3. Spot the fake: vote out the imposter before they blend in.

No download and no sign-up needed. Play as a guest on your phone or computer, and share the room link with your friends.
